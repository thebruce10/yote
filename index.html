Yoté

Instructions:

Each player starts with 12 tokens in hand and players alternate turns, with white going first.
On a turn a player may either:

  • Place a token in an empty space.
  • Move one of their tokens orthogonally to an empty adjacent cell.
  • Capture an opponents orthogonally adjacent token by jumping over it into the empty cell immediately beyond it. Remove the captured token and choose another of the opponents tokens to remove.

The player who captures all the opponents tokens is the winner. If both players have less than 3 tokens left, they may agree to end the game in a draw.
